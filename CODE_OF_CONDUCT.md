# Code of conduct

We want people of all backgrounds and experience levels to be able to contribute to this
project. This policy applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, other project spaces,
and communications in which someone represents the project.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific and constructive. Critique code and ideas rather than people.
- Make room for different experience levels and perspectives.
- Respect privacy, consent and the boundaries set by other participants.
- Accept correction and take responsibility for the effects of your behavior.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, sexual attention or imagery, sustained
disruption, and publishing someone's private information without consent are not acceptable.
Retaliation against someone who raises a concern or participates in an investigation is also
not acceptable.

## Private reports

Report conduct concerns to **engineering-safety@coveragecat.com**. Include relevant context
and links where possible, but do not post private reports in public issues. Maintainers will
limit disclosure to what is needed to investigate and respond.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content and may warn, temporarily restrict, or
remove a participant, depending on the severity and history of the behavior. They should
explain a moderation decision to the affected participant when it is safe and practical.

You may request reconsideration at the same private address. A maintainer directly involved
in a reported incident should not be the sole person deciding its outcome when another
maintainer is available.

This policy applies to maintainers as well as other participants.
